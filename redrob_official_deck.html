My Solution

Deterministic, CPU-only candidate ranking pipeline

Rule-based scoring across 6 signal groups — fully transparent, reproducible, and explainable at any reviewer stage.

  • Skills match via token overlap with the JD
  • Years of experience extraction and scoring
  • Seniority-level alignment with the target role
  • Bangalore location bonus (JD preference)
  • Notice-period penalty for long lead times
  • All 23 redrob behavioral signals weighted
Step 1 Load candidates.jsonl (100,000 records — local only, not committed) Step 2 Extract 6 signal groups per candidate Step 3 Apply JD-priority weights → composite final_score (0.0 – 1.0) Step 4 Sort descending, take top 100 Step 5 Write submission.csv with rank, score, 1-2 sentence reasoning
Constraints satisfied: CPU-only · no network during ranking · ≤ 5 min · ≤ 16 GB RAM 3 / 5
